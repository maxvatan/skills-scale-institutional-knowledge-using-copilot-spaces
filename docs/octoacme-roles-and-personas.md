# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers / Deployment Owners

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and deployment so changes reach users safely and predictably.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release timing, readiness checks, and deployment activities
- Confirm rollback or mitigation plans and post-deployment verification
- Communicate release status and coordinate follow-up actions

### Collaboration
Coordinate with the PM on milestones and risks, the Product Manager on scope and release communication, Developers on deployment readiness, QA/Testing on validation, and Stakeholders on timing and outcomes.

### Lifecycle Fit
Planning, release, and post-release verification.

---

## Business Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Business Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business needs, provide decision support, and help ensure the project delivers intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business goals, constraints, and success measures
- Provide timely decisions on priorities, scope, and trade-offs
- Review progress and confirm whether outcomes meet business needs

### Collaboration
Align with the PM on decisions and escalations, the Product Manager on value and priorities, Developers and QA/Testing on feasibility and evidence, and other Stakeholders on shared expectations.

### Lifecycle Fit
Initiation and planning, with decision points and outcome reviews throughout delivery.

---

## Tech Leads / Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Tech Leads guide technical direction and help the engineering team deliver maintainable solutions that meet product needs.

### Responsibilities
- Translate requirements into technical approaches and implementation guidance
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and estimates
- Support design reviews, quality practices, and knowledge sharing

### Collaboration
Work with the PM on estimates, dependencies, and risks; the Product Manager on requirements and trade-offs; Developers on design and implementation; QA/Testing on testability; and Stakeholders on technical impacts when needed.

### Lifecycle Fit
Planning and execution, with technical input during release readiness.

---

## QA Leads / Test Owners

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and coordinate validation so the team can assess quality against acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done.

### Responsibilities
- Shape the test strategy and identify coverage needs
- Coordinate test execution, defect triage, and quality reporting
- Confirm acceptance criteria are verifiable and communicate residual risks

### Collaboration
Coordinate with the PM on quality milestones and risks, the Product Manager on acceptance criteria, Developers on testability and defect resolution, QA/Testing peers on coverage and execution, and Stakeholders on acceptance evidence and known limitations.

### Lifecycle Fit
Planning and execution, with final validation before release.

---

## Scrum Masters / Delivery Facilitators

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate the team’s delivery practices and help maintain a sustainable, transparent flow of work.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team surface and resolve process impediments
- Support clear work tracking and continuous improvement

### Collaboration
Partner with the PM on delivery visibility and dependencies, the Product Manager on backlog readiness, Developers and QA/Testing on team ceremonies and impediments, and Stakeholders on reviews and feedback.

### Lifecycle Fit
Planning, execution, and retrospectives.

---

## Support / Operations Liaisons

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Liaisons connect delivery teams with the people who operate and support the product after release.

### Responsibilities
- Identify operational readiness needs, including runbooks and monitoring
- Coordinate support preparation and incident escalation paths
- Share production issues and user feedback with the delivery team

### Collaboration
Work with the PM on operational dependencies and readiness, the Product Manager on support trends and user impact, Developers on observability and fixes, QA/Testing on operational scenarios, and Stakeholders on release impact and follow-up.

### Lifecycle Fit
Planning, release readiness, production support, and continuous improvement.
